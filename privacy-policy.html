Privacy Policy — Gurbani Saanjh (ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਸਾਂਝ)

Last updated: 11 November 2026
App: Gurbani Saanjh, package com.gurbanisaanjh.app, version 2.2.54
Contact: karmjitgill88@gmail.com

Short version

Gurbani Saanjh reads offline. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics. Your reading progress, settings, and saved items stay on your phone. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The one exception is the Ask AI feature (below): when you ask a question, only the question text is sent to our AI server so it can answer — nothing else about you is sent.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your settings (language, text size, translation choices, reading preferences).
  • Your reading progress, saved Shabads, and search history — stored only on your phone.
  • Nothing else is sent anywhere. The only server we run is the small Ask AI server described below.

What leaves your phone

  • Daily Hukamnama: once a day the app asks BaniDB (api.banidb.com) for today's Hukamnama, using only the date. No personal information is sent.
  • Ask AI (on by default, no key needed): when you ask a question, the app sends only your question text to our shared AI server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Google Gemini to get the answer. No name, email, account, location, or other personal information is sent — only the question. There are no AI settings in this Play Store version, and no API key inside the app. If you never use Ask, nothing is ever sent for AI.
  • Voice search: uses your phone's built-in speech recognition. Audio is handled by your device / its speech service, not by us.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only for voice search, only while you are speaking into it.
  • Notifications — only for the daily 5:00 AM Hukamnama reminder, sent from your phone itself.
  • Internet — only for the daily Hukamnama fetch and for the Ask AI answer.

Children

The app contains Sikh scripture and is suitable for general audiences. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
